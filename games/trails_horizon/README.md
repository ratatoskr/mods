# Trails beyond the Horizon

For The Legend of Heroes: Trails beyond the Horizon (Kai no Kiseki).

- Wraith's free camera moves the game's camera.
- Wraith's posing works for the characters in the game, listed by their names from the game's name table.
- Wraith's game speed controls how fast the game runs, allowing you to speed it up, slow it down, or pause it.
- **Clean shot**, on Photo mode's toolbar, hides the HUD and menus, and the names and markers over people, for screenshots.
- Photo mode's camera has **Lens**, with near clip and shift, and **Look**, with the game's own depth of field (focus where the camera looks or at a distance, sharp range and blur size) and its black and white, negative and screen blur filters.
- Other plugins' `threads.onTick` runs on the game's own thread, while you're playing.
- Tweaks, under headings in the Tweaks window:
  - **Money & points**: change Mira, G-Tokens, Medals, Sepith and gourmet points, and see play time.
  - **Story**: change the L.G.C. alignment, Connect and SP.
  - **Money**: **Free shopping** makes buying cost nothing, and **Give Mira** adds Mira, 10,000 by default.
  - **Items**: **Spawn item** gives you any item from the game's item list. **Items aren't used up** leaves you with as many after using or selling them, though Sepith still gets spent. **Full stacks** fills an item's stack when you pick one up, leaving a little room so more can still be picked up.
  - **Experience**: battles give 10 times the experience and hollow core experience, and new dishes twice the gourmet points, or however many times you set.
  - **Combat**: **One-hit kills**, **Party takes no damage** and **Party can't die**, which leaves the party on 1 HP. **Restore HP, EP and CP** tops up everyone.
  - **Party**: **Party members** opens the Party window, also under the plugin's button on the overlay's bar. It lists each route's party in order with who leads, attacks, supports or is in reserve. Drag one onto another to swap them, or right-click for leader, up, down and take out. The picked member's flags match the old Cheat Engine table's: how they joined (normal, guest or extra), what they're locked to (reserve or the lineup), equipment locked and forced attacker. Anyone in the save can be added from a searchable list, and Shift+click types in any id. Changes go through the game's own party code and show on the field straight away, no menu needed.
  - **Field**: enemies don't notice you, tailing without being seen, and FIO's lasers don't see her.
  - **Fishing**: always Excellent, always rank A, **Catch in one hit**, which keeps the reel meter nearly full so the next hit lands the fish, and **Pick the fish**, which makes whatever bites the fish you pick from the game's fish list, sized for it, or the biggest it gets.
  - **Grim Garten**: stage objectives are always met, and rank missions are done in one go.
  - **Misc**: instant hacking, and unlocking achievements without their requirements.
